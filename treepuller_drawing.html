Heath Tree Extraction Rig — Component Layout

Side elevation, cutaway. Cut stems are winched out of the peat on an aluminium sled and transferred to the truck bed. Haul line (2, 10, 11) highlighted in orange; buried and in-body parts shown as hidden detail.

1Trucksmall flatbed; anchor mass and transport
2Pulleyelectric winch drum, motor and fairlead
3Batterytraction pack under the bed, feeds the winch
4Computerhaul controller in the cab
5Ground anchorspade driven into the peat, takes the pull
6Load bedstake rack; stems transferred off the sled
7Sledaluminium boat, floats the load over soft ground
8Runnersupturned skids, low drag on heather and peat
9Bunkbolsters and stakes holding the stems on deck
10Chokerchain binding the load to the bunk
11Cablemain line, fairlead to sled bridle
12Cameramast-mounted, watches the haul path
13Remoteoperator handheld, manual override
